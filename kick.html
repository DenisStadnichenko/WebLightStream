Kick Integration

StadFlow now supports streaming to Kick.com. Reach your audience on the fastest-growing streaming platform with ease.

Features

  • High-bitrate RTMP streaming to Kick
  • Easy setup with Stream Key
  • Low-latency performance

How to connect

1. Open StadFlow app.

2. Select Kick as your destination.

3. Copy your Stream Key from the Kick Creator Dashboard.

4. Paste and start streaming!
