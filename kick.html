Kick.com Integration

Stream to the fastest-growing platform with high bitrate and low latency.

How to connect

StadFlow connects to Kick natively via OAuth — no stream key needed:

  1. In StadFlow, tap Add Account → Kick.
  2. Sign in to Kick in the browser and allow access.
  3. You are redirected back to StadFlow automatically. If the app does not open, tap Open StadFlow on the page.

StadFlow fetches your ingest URL and stream key from Kick after sign-in and stores them encrypted on the device.

Using a stream key instead

Prefer a stream key? Add Kick as a Custom RTMP destination:

  1. Open your Kick Creator Dashboard → Settings → Stream Key.
  2. Copy the Stream URL and Stream Key.
  3. In StadFlow, tap Add Account → Custom, paste both values and save.

Recommended settings

Kick accepts high bitrates. On a good connection use 1080p / 60 FPS / 8 Mbps; on mobile data start with 720p / 30 FPS / 2.5 Mbps.

Tip: you can add several Kick accounts and stream to them at the same time.
