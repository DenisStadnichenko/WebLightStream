Twitch Integration

Connect your Twitch account in one tap and start streaming with pro-level quality.

Key Features

  • Native OAuth Login: Secure connection without sharing your password.
  • Ingest Selection: Automatically chooses the best Twitch server for your location.
  • Chat Support: Integrated chat overlay and notifications (Pro feature).
  • Stream Info: Update your title and category directly from the app.

Troubleshooting

If you experience issues connecting to Twitch:

  1. Ensure you have Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) enabled on your Twitch account.
  2. Check if your Stream Key hasn't been reset in the Twitch dashboard.
  3. Try re-authenticating by removing and adding the account again in StadFlow.
