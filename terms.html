3. Subscriptions and Payment

PRO is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Google Play. It is offered on two billing periods: $9.99 per month and $49.99 per year. Google converts the price for your country, so the amount charged may differ from the figures above.

Payment is charged to your Google account when you confirm the purchase, and the subscription renews automatically at the end of every billing period unless you cancel it beforehand. Google Play takes the renewal payment within 24 hours of the end of the current period.

To cancel, open Google Play and go to Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions, then cancel StadFlow. Cancelling stops future renewals; it does not shorten the period you have already paid for. PRO features stay available until that period ends, after which the app returns to the free plan. A broadcast that is already running is not interrupted when a subscription ends.

Refunds are handled by Google Play under Google's refund policy. We do not process payments and cannot issue refunds ourselves; payment details are never passed to us.

We may change the subscription price. Google Play notifies existing subscribers of a price change before it takes effect and asks for consent where its rules require it. If you do not accept the new price, you can cancel before the change applies.

4. Promo Codes

A promo code grants PRO for a set number of days. Each code works once and has a last day on which it can be activated; after that day it no longer works. Codes are entered on the Premium screen in the app. If you enter a second code while PRO is still running, its days are added to the time you have left.

Promo codes have no monetary value, cannot be exchanged for cash, and cannot be sold or transferred. A period granted by a promo code does not renew automatically and is not a subscription: when the days run out, the app returns to the free plan.
