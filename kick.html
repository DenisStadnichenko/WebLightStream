How to Connect

StadFlow supports Kick via custom RTMP destinations. Follow these steps to go live:

  1. Go to your Kick Creator Dashboard.
  2. Select Settings → Stream Key.
  3. Copy your Stream URL and Stream Key.
  4. In StadFlow, tap Add Account → Custom RTMP.
  5. Paste your credentials and tap Save.

Pro Tip: Native Kick API integration is coming soon in our next update!
