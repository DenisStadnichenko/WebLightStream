YouTube Integration

Sign in with Google, pick a channel and go live. StadFlow creates and manages the broadcast for you.

How to connect

  1. In StadFlow, tap Add Account → YouTube.
  2. Choose the Google account that owns your channel and allow StadFlow to manage your YouTube live streams.
  3. The channel appears in your destinations list. Repeat for every channel you want to stream to — there is no limit on the number of accounts.

What StadFlow does for you

  • Creates a live broadcast with your title and privacy setting (public, unlisted or private) when you tap Start Stream.
  • Binds the stream to the broadcast and switches it live automatically.
  • Ends the broadcast when you stop streaming so the VOD is saved to your channel.
  • Refreshes the sign-in tokens in the background — you only log in once.

Requirements

  • Live streaming must be enabled on your channel. YouTube requires a verified phone number and a 24‑hour waiting period after the first activation.
  • Mobile live streaming on YouTube has no subscriber requirement when using a third-party encoder like StadFlow.

Troubleshooting

  • "Live streaming is not enabled" — open YouTube Studio → Go live and complete the activation, then wait 24 hours.
  • Broadcast stays "Waiting" — check your bitrate; on mobile networks start with 720p / 2.5 Mbps.
  • Wrong channel — brand channels have their own Google identity; pick the brand account during sign-in.

StadFlow's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Tokens are stored encrypted on your device only.
