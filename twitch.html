Twitch Integration

Connect your Twitch account in one tap and start streaming with pro-level quality.

How to connect

  1. In StadFlow, tap Add Account → Twitch.
  2. Sign in to Twitch in the browser and click Authorize.
  3. You are redirected back to StadFlow automatically. If the app does not open, tap Open StadFlow on the page.

StadFlow asks only for permission to read your stream key and basic profile. You can add as many Twitch accounts as you need and enable them per stream.

Key features

  • Native OAuth login — secure connection without sharing your password or stream key.
  • Automatic stream key — fetched from Twitch after sign-in; you never have to copy it.
  • Token refresh — log in once, StadFlow keeps the session alive.
  • Multiple accounts — stream to your main and backup channels at the same time.

Troubleshooting

  1. Twitch requires Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) to be enabled on your account before you can stream. Enable it at twitch.tv/settings/security.
  2. If you reset your stream key in the Twitch dashboard, remove and re-add the account in StadFlow.
  3. If the browser does not return you to the app, make sure StadFlow is allowed to open links (Android Settings → Apps → StadFlow → Open by default).
