Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find an answer? Check the Help Center or contact us.

Can I stream to multiple YouTube channels?

Yes. Add several YouTube accounts and enable the channels you need. StadFlow supports multiple OAuth accounts simultaneously.

Can I use Twitch and Kick at the same time?

Yes. StadFlow is designed for simultaneous streaming to multiple destinations. You can mix and match any platforms.

Can I stream to my own server?

Yes. Use custom RTMP, RTSP, SRT or UDP destinations. Just provide the server URL and stream key in the destination settings.

What is the maximum resolution supported?

StadFlow streams at up to 1080p at 60 FPS with a video bitrate of up to 8 Mbps. Use 720p / 30 FPS on mobile networks for the most stable stream.

Does StadFlow support internal audio capture?

Yes. On Android 10 and newer StadFlow can capture system audio when streaming your screen, and mix it with the microphone.

What is the difference between Free and Pro?

Free gives you a whole stream: 2 destinations at once, 2 layers in the picture, one chat account per platform, the panel over the game, recording and the full quality range, with ads. PRO removes the limits and the ads. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

Is there a watermark?

No. StadFlow never adds a watermark to your stream, on any plan.

Can I record the stream locally?

Yes. Turn on "Save stream" in Settings and every broadcast is also saved to the Movies/StadFlow folder on your device.

Which Android version do I need?

Android 8.0 (API 26) or newer. Internal audio capture requires Android 10 or newer.

Does the stream stop when I lock the phone?

No. StadFlow runs as a foreground service and keeps streaming while the screen is off or you use other apps. Disable battery optimisation for StadFlow to be safe.

What are layers and the scene mixer?

Layers are the things laid over your picture: your camera, the chat, a web overlay (alerts, goal bars, timers) and a cover for "Starting soon" or "Be right back". You drag, resize and reorder them in the mixer. Each layer can be shown to the viewers, to you, or to both — so your screen can stay clean while the viewers see everything.

Can I read and answer chat while playing?

Yes. YouTube and Twitch chat is read inside the app, can be shown as a layer and answered from the panel over the game. Kick chat is not supported yet — streaming to Kick works, only its chat has no source.

What is the panel over the game?

A compact floating panel that stays over your gameplay: start and stop the stream, mute the microphone, see the health of every destination and read the chat without leaving the game. It works on the free plan.

Will my phone survive a long stream?

The bitrate follows your network instead of demanding a fixed number, and the app watches the phone's temperature and battery. You are warned before the picture starts to stutter, and the quality steps down on its own rather than letting the stream die.

Can I control the camera manually?

Yes. The Pro camera panel gives you exposure and exposure lock, ISO and shutter, focus distance, white balance as presets or in kelvin, anti-flicker, preview stabilisation, noise reduction and sharpness. If your lens does not support a setting, the app says so instead of pretending. It is available on the free plan.

Where are my passwords and stream keys stored?

You never enter platform passwords in StadFlow — sign-in happens in the browser via OAuth. Tokens and stream keys are stored encrypted on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

Why is the app not on Google Play?

StadFlow is currently distributed as a direct APK download while we prepare the Google Play release. The APK on this site is always the latest build.
