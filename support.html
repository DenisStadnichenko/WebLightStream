Getting started

  1. Install the app. Download stadflow.apk and open it. Android will ask you to allow installation from this source — confirm it. StadFlow requires Android 8.0 or newer.
  2. Add a destination. Tap Add Account and choose YouTube, Twitch, Kick or Custom. Sign in via the browser or paste a stream key. See Account setup below.
  3. Choose a source. Pick the camera (front or rear), screen capture or a video file from your device. The app asks for the camera, microphone or screen-recording permission only for the source you actually use.
  4. Set the quality. Open Settings and pick a resolution (480p, 720p or 1080p), frame rate (30 or 60 FPS) and video bitrate (1, 2.5, 5 or 8 Mbps). Start with 720p / 30 FPS / 2.5 Mbps if you are on mobile data.
  5. Go live. Enable the destinations you want for this stream and tap Start Stream. A persistent notification shows the stream status; you can keep using your phone while streaming.

Tip: turn on Save stream in Settings to keep a local copy of every broadcast. Recordings are stored in Movies/StadFlow on your device.

Account setup

YouTube

StadFlow signs in with your Google account via OAuth and creates broadcasts for you — no stream key needed. You can add several Google accounts and pick which channels go live each time. Full YouTube guide →

Twitch

Tap Add Account → Twitch, authorize in the browser and you will be sent back to the app automatically. Twitch requires two-factor authentication to be enabled on your account before you can stream. Full Twitch guide →

Kick

Tap Add Account → Kick and sign in. StadFlow fetches your stream key automatically. You can also add Kick as a custom RTMP destination if you prefer. Full Kick guide →

Custom RTMP / RTMPS / RTSP / SRT / UDP

Tap Add Account → Custom, give the destination a name and paste the server URL and stream key from your provider. Typical formats:

ProtocolURL example
RTMPrtmp://live.example.com/app + stream key
RTMPSrtmps://live.example.com:443/app + stream key
SRTsrt://ingest.example.com:9000?streamid=YOUR_ID
RTSPrtsp://server.example.com:8554/stream
UDPudp://192.168.1.10:5000

This is how you stream to Restream, Facebook Live, Trovo, VK Video, OBS on a PC or your own media server. Stream keys are stored encrypted on the device and are never shown in full once saved.

Quality & troubleshooting

Recommended settings

ConnectionResolutionFPSBitrate
4G / weak Wi‑Fi720p301–2.5 Mbps
5G / good Wi‑Fi1080p30–605 Mbps
Fast Wi‑Fi / Ethernet1080p608 Mbps

When streaming to several destinations at once, the same encoded video is sent to each one, so your upload must handle bitrate × number of destinations.

Stream keeps dropping or freezing

  • Lower the bitrate one step and switch to 30 FPS.
  • Disable battery optimisation for StadFlow in Android settings so the system does not pause the stream in the background.
  • StadFlow reconnects to each destination automatically when the connection is lost; if a platform still shows you offline, stop and start the stream.

No sound or wrong audio source

  • Check the audio source in Settings: microphone, internal audio or Bluetooth headset.
  • Internal (system) audio capture is available on Android 10 and newer and only for the screen source.
  • Some apps and DRM-protected content block internal audio capture — this is an Android restriction.

Twitch or Kick login fails

  • Make sure the browser returned you to StadFlow. If not, tap Open StadFlow on the authorization page.
  • Remove the account in StadFlow and add it again to refresh the tokens.
  • Twitch: enable 2FA on your Twitch account, it is required for streaming.

Free plan limits

The free plan lets you stream to 2 destinations at once. You can unlock a third one temporarily by watching an ad, or upgrade to Pro for unlimited destinations and no ads.
