Kick.com Integration

Stream to the fastest-growing platform with high bitrate and low latency.

How to Connect

StadFlow connects to Kick natively via OAuth — no stream key needed:

  1. In StadFlow, tap Add Account → Kick.
  2. Sign in to Kick in the browser and allow access.
  3. You will be redirected back to StadFlow automatically.

Prefer a stream key? You can still add Kick as a Custom RTMP destination using the URL and key from your Kick Creator Dashboard.

Tip: you can add several Kick accounts and stream to them at the same time.
