Frequently Asked Questions

Can I stream to multiple YouTube channels?

Yes. Add several YouTube accounts and enable the channels you need. StadFlow supports multiple OAuth accounts simultaneously.

Can I use Twitch and Kick at the same time?

Yes. StadFlow is designed for simultaneous streaming to multiple destinations. You can mix and match any platforms.

Can I stream to my own server?

Yes. Use custom RTMP, RTSP, SRT or UDP destinations. Just provide the server URL and stream key in the destination settings.

What is the maximum resolution supported?

StadFlow supports up to 4K (2160p) resolution at 60 FPS, depending on your device's hardware capabilities and internet connection.

Does StadFlow support internal audio capture?

Yes, on Android 10 and above, StadFlow can capture internal system audio. You can also mix it with your microphone input.

Is there a watermark in the free version?

The free version includes a small "Streamed with StadFlow" watermark. You can remove it by upgrading to the Pro version.
