Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

At StadFlow, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Data: We do not collect personal identification information such as your name or address. When you connect to third-party services (YouTube, Twitch, Kick), we use OAuth tokens to facilitate the connection. These tokens are stored securely on your device.

Usage Data: We may collect non-personal information about how you use the app, such as crash reports and performance metrics, to improve the quality of our service.

Ads (Free plan): The free version of the app may show ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use the advertising ID of your device to show relevant ads; you can reset it or opt out of personalised ads in Android settings. See the Google advertising policy.

Local recordings: If you enable "Save stream", recordings are written to the Movies/StadFlow folder on your device only. We never receive them.

Media Data: StadFlow requires access to your camera and microphone to provide streaming services. This data is processed locally and streamed directly to your chosen destinations. We do not record or store your streams on our servers.

2. Use of Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the application.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand the application features.
  • Understand and analyze how you use the application.
  • Develop new products, services, features, and functionality.

3. Third-Party Services

Our application integrates with third-party services. By using these integrations, you agree to their respective privacy policies:

  • Google/YouTube Privacy Policy
  • Twitch Privacy Notice
  • Kick Privacy Policy

StadFlow's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

All tokens and stream keys are stored only on your device and are deleted when you remove the account in the app or uninstall StadFlow. You can also revoke access at any time from your Google, Twitch or Kick account security settings.

5. Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your information. While we have taken reasonable steps to secure the information you provide, please be aware that despite our efforts, no security measures are perfect or impenetrable.
